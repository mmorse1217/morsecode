## About

Morse Code is a specialist research engineering company focused on computational systems. We design, build, and optimize systems with demanding computational requirements, with expertise in numerical algorithms, high-performance computing, GPU performance, and ML systems. We collaborate closely with clients to determine if and how improved computation can help them achieve their goals and work together on a solution, taking research prototypes through to production.

Morse Code was founded by Matt Morse, a research engineer with over a decade of experience working on computational systems in research and production settings. He has a computer science PhD from NYU Courant and has worked in AI research and quantitative finance.
